❓ Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about orders, shipping, and returns.

Q: How can I track my order?

A: You can easily track your orders by clicking on "Track Orders" in the footer or by visiting your Profile -> My Orders section.

Q: What is the return policy?

A: We offer a 7-day hassle-free return and replacement policy on all eligible products.

Q: How do I contact customer support?

A: You can raise a support ticket directly from any of your order details pages for quick assistance.

Q: Are the products on BloomCart 100% genuine and original?

A: Yes, absolutely! We source all our products directly from verified manufacturers, authorized brands, and trusted distributors to ensure you receive 100% authentic and high-quality items.

Q: Is my payment and personal information secure on this website?

A: Your security is our top priority. All online transactions are processed through highly secure, 256-bit encrypted gateways (like Razorpay & UPI). We never store your sensitive credit card or banking passwords on our servers.

Q: How long does delivery usually take?

A: Standard delivery usually takes between 2 to 5 business days depending on your location. If you are a Prime member, you unlock express priority shipping options!

Q: How do refunds work and when will I get my money back?

A: Once your returned item is received and inspected at our warehouse, your refund is processed instantly. It typically reflects in your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.

Q: Can I cancel my order after placing it?

A: Yes, you can cancel your order directly from your "My Orders" page before it has been shipped out for delivery.
